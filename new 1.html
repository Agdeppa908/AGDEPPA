Shangri-La Boracay

Boracay is a beautiful island in the Visayas known for its white sand beaches, turquoise water and unforgettable island adventures.

LOCATION Aklan, Philippines
ACTIVITY Beach / Island Hopping

Palawan's Underground River

The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River is a UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring an underground river flowing through a spectacular limestone cave.

LOCATION Palawan, Philippines
ACTIVITY River Tour / Adventure
Explore Now ↗

Chocolate Hills

More than a thousand symmetrical cone-shaped hills cover Bohol's countryside. During the dry season, the hills turn chocolate brown.

LOCATION Bohol, Philippines
ACTIVITY Sightseeing / Photography
Explore Now ↗

Mayon Volcano

Located in Albay, Mayon Volcano is famous for its near-perfect cone shape and spectacular scenery. It is one of the Philippines' most recognizable natural landmarks.

LOCATION Albay, Philippines
ACTIVITY Hiking / Photography
Explore Now ↗

Siargao Island

Known as the surfing capital of the Philippines, Siargao attracts travelers with Cloud 9, beautiful lagoons, island hopping tours and its relaxed island atmosphere.

LOCATION Surigao del Norte
ACTIVITY Surfing / Island Hopping
